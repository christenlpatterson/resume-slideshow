Projects and context · Visa conversational BI agent

Grounding an agent in curated knowledge, not guesses

Graph RAG, not vanilla RAGTradeoff: more upfront modeling and infrastructure. Why: the agent reasons over entities and relationships, so answers are easier to explain and debug.
Curated metadata, not inferred tagsTradeoff: slower to scale, needs stewardship. Why: the agent reflects how the business actually talks, which reduces hallucination and policy risk.
Humans stay in the loopA knowledge-graph editor for data stewards, plus human-oversight and evaluation patterns, balanced automation with review.

~40% → ~80%

Response accuracy: broad Data Catalog context vs bounded, curated knowledge-graph context. The result put Knowledge Graphs on the product roadmap.

What I delivered

Designed and prototyped a GraphRAG pipeline that semantically matches questions to curated graph context and injects it before response generation. Also prototyped an LLM eval of accuracy and latency. The delivery team launched the internal product.
